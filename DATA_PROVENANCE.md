# Data provenance and extraction record

## Experimental observations on ball python scales

The 48 h bacterial-colonisation observations are taken from Figure 5 of Peroutka et al. (2026), cited in `manuscript/refs.bib` as `Peroutka2026`.

- Figure 5(a) reports scale-pair CFU as a percentage of the polystyrene control: 11.8 ± 1.3% for *Escherichia coli* and 22.4 ± 8.3% for *Staphylococcus aureus* (mean ± s.d., three biological replicates). The manuscript converts these ratios to log reductions and standard errors by the delta method.
- Figure 5(b) reports log10 CFU cm−2 for polystyrene and scales. The analysis repeats the endpoint calculations using those values. Because the polystyrene control has no reported error bar, its s.d. is assumed equal to the corresponding scale s.d.; this assumption is stated in the supplementary material.
- SEM-derived following-tip spacing, adjacent-tip spacing and projected blade length use the reported measurements and standard deviations. Blade width, taper and pit dimensions are estimates from the published high-magnification micrograph. Tip height and pit depth are unmeasured in the top-view micrographs and are assigned broad uniform priors.

## External Sharklet AF comparison

Short-term static-attachment observations are taken from May et al. (2014) and Xu et al. (2017), cited as `May2014` and `Xu2017`. The values and their transformations are defined in `stats_v2.py`. The Sharklet comparison is external to the Peroutka-derived blade-array ensemble and does not recalibrate the model.

## Generated data

The following are generated by the scripts in this repository:

- 40 blade-array reconstructions and their species-specific simulation outputs;
- grid, time-step and periodic-domain verification outputs;
- mechanism-knockout, design-map, material-adhesion and time-course simulations;
- geometric and mechanistic Saltelli/Sobol evaluation tables;
- bootstrap endpoint-map summaries and prediction intervals;
- manuscript tables, numerical macros and figures.

All stochastic stages use explicit seeds in `analysis_v2.py` and `stats_v2.py`. The geometric Sobol design uses uniform sampling over each parameter's bounded support. The production ensemble uses the parameter-specific truncated-normal or uniform priors in `GEOM_PRIOR`.

No new bacterial experiments, animal procedures or human-participant data were generated for this study.
